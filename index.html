🌾 About Village AI

Village AI is being built as a local digital companion for village communities. The goal is simple: make AI easy to use while creating a place where local information, local needs and local people can come together.

AI answers should be checked for important decisions. Local information will only be truly reliable when the Village AI backend is connected to verified local data.
